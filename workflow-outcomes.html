Research workflows and their outcomes

Drafted v0.1 · October 27, 2026 · first calculation, 233 papers · paper codebook 1.5.0 · exploratory

  • Question. Do the workflow features coded from a paper (where it starts, which modes and graph nodes it uses, how often it loops back, how it checks its result) go with its outcomes: impact, novelty, breadth, advance?
  • Short answer. Weakly, and mostly in expected directions. Of 425 feature-outcome tests, 15 pass a false-discovery threshold (q < 0.1); the largest correlations are 0.2 to 0.6. Advance is not measured yet.
  • Scope. Results only; codings and papers stay in the lab’s private repository.

  • Paper length check. Longer papers have more steps and more citations. Controlling for reference count, the impact correlations drop but stay positive: steps 0.17, revisits 0.15, decisive simulation 0.19.
  • Partly circular. Imports from other fields and references outside geoscience measure overlapping things; the 0.57 says the coder’s import list tracks the reference list, more than it says anything about outcomes.
  • Breadth of citers (53 papers): no association passes the threshold. The largest is the claim gate (exposing the claim to a test it could have failed), 0.37 [0.11, 0.59], q = 0.11.

Reading the result

  • Work that loops back more and is decided by simulation is cited somewhat more; work decided by a method, or that starts from a new capability, draws more on other fields; work that combines many modes or synthesizes draws less on other fields.
  • None of this is causal. Journal, field, open access, team size and topic all differ between papers and are not controlled here.
  • Novelty as measured (a rare topic pair) barely relates to any workflow feature. Either the proxy is weak or novelty does not show in the workflow; the human ratings will tell which.

Limits

  • One model coder (Claude Opus 5.5); test-retest node overlap 0.86 on 50 papers; no human check yet.
  • 233 of 525 eligible papers, open-access-leaning; 2022 papers, citations at about four years.
  • Proxies: novelty and breadth are bibliometric stand-ins; advance is missing.
  • 425 tests: some of the 15 will be false positives (by construction about 1 to 2 at q < 0.1).
