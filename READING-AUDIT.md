# Reading audit, v0.3

The required set is 13 two-paper pairs. Supplementary readings remain optional; historical publication dates are not replaced by digitization dates.

v0.3 changes two meetings. The former architecture and evaluation meetings are merged into meeting 13, which frees a slot for a new meeting 12 on scientific polymathy. The merged meeting keeps two required papers per participant and moves the two critiques that were previously required to a discussant-led role, so no participant's reading load increases. Entries below are v0.2 rationale except where marked.

## 1. Is there a scientific method?

Retain the original pair, but explicitly distinguish methodological arguments from evidence about research practice.

## 2. Exploratory experimentation is not theory-free

Complete the Steinle citation and add a genuine qualification from Karaca rather than leaving a one-paper session.

## 3. From coastal traces to earthquake histories

Replace the repeat Cleland assignment and broad Siever essay with two primary Cascadia papers.

## 4. Plate tectonics: concept formation and a discriminating test

Replace a broad Dietz retrospective with primary concept-and-test papers. Move the ocean-drilling overview to the field-practice meeting.

## 5. New sensing capability versus trustworthy measurement

Replace an indirect seismic-bulletin handbook chapter and a general measurement essay with a concrete geophysical instrument/calibration pair.

## 6. Explore freely; distinguish exploration from confirmation

Replace an insufficiently specified edited-volume chapter with a precise methodological paper; preserve exploration rather than demand preregistration of all discovery.

## 7. Field and marine research under real constraints

Keep the close-to-practice Arctic history and pair it with the relocated ocean-drilling paper; remove a general book-chapter substitute.

## 8. Serendipity, anomaly, and scientific interestingness

Give Moore a substantive companion rather than treating a first-person narrative as a general theory of discovery.

## 9. Hypothesis testing when several models fit

Remove the unfinished companion citation and redundant Platt assignment. Recall Platt from Meeting 1 while using a concrete Earth-system model pair.

## 10. Scientific novelty: new to whom, compared with what?

Retain the core pair; place human-review and agent-benchmark papers in optional extensions. A search miss is not proof of historical priority.

## 11. Scientific advance: cumulative work and field-shaping change

Retain the Wu-Petersen methodological pairing as the required pair. Keep progress definitions, gatekeeping, and delayed recognition as optional extensions.

## 12. Scientific polymathy: breadth, specialization, and borrowed competence

New in v0.3. The group's working hypothesis is that agents make individual polymathy practical again. It is entered as a hypothesis with a stated disconfirming observation, not as a premise, and the pair deliberately predates the technology so the terms are set before it is introduced. Burke supplies the historical retrospective; note that a prosopography of recognized polymaths is selected on success and cannot establish how often breadth failed. Kitcher supplies the argument that specialization is a rational collective strategy rather than a decline, which is the strongest available objection to the hypothesis on its own terms. Trust and epistemic dependence, present-day career costs of breadth, and a measurement of epistemic diversity in language models are optional extensions rather than a fourth and fifth required paper.

## 13. What architecture follows from practice, and what would show it worked?

Merged in v0.3 from the v0.2 meetings 12 and 13. The required pair is the implemented system and the concrete benchmark, so that architecture and evaluation are specified in the same hour and no capability is proposed without the evidence that would check it. Messeri and Crockett and Flake and Fried, previously required, become discussant-led: each is taken in depth by one rotating discussant and cited on the page, and both stay in the reading library. Messick remains optional. The merge is an editorial compression, not a judgment that the critiques matter less.

No journal PDFs are bundled. The original publication or proceedings, rather than a preprint or blog summary, is the cited version wherever identified. Book chapters and first-person accounts are labeled according to their evidential role, not silently treated as controlled studies of research practice.
