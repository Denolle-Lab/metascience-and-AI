## 2. Corrections from Astra's second report that I accept

Each with the change it makes to my position.

1. **Breiman.** ETAS, Gutenberg-Richter fits, and hazard models are stochastic data models, Breiman's first culture, not his algorithmic second. I mislabeled. Breiman and Shmueli enter as two lenses on statistical practice, not as a taxonomy of the statistical mode.
2. **Precedents.** Operational columns are easier to code with; that does not make them more adequate. The glossary's columns are a contribution to test, not a superiority.
3. **The breadth recipe.** Moderate distance, conventional core, fast fields, and outsiders come from four studies with four constructs, never tested jointly. They are covariates for the corpus study and separate findings for the page, not one prescription. Shi and Evans analyze surprise in contents and in contexts; I compressed it.
4. **Costs are not disconfirmers.** Lower productivity and funding success are costs or selection mechanisms. They do not disconfirm the epistemic claim.
5. **Liu et al.** measure topic diversity then concentration across a career, not exploratory experiments then discriminating tests. It motivates a portfolio policy, not a routing rule.
6. **Azoulay et al.** is an observational comparison with matching and difference-in-differences, and the authors say so. It is informative about funding arrangements; it is not a natural experiment on an action gate.
7. **CASP.** It shows that an independent blind assessment made an advance demonstrable. It does not isolate the gate's contribution from data, architecture, and compute, and it supplies reference structures, not an exact verifier. I also claimed seismology has no comparable infrastructure for source studies. That is wrong: the Source Inversion Validation project is a blind benchmark for finite-fault inversion (Mai et al., 2016), and SeisBench benchmarks phase picking (Woollam et al., 2022), beside CSEP for forecasting (Schorlemmer et al., 2018). What is missing, as far as a scoped search today shows, is a prospective test for velocity-change and tomographic claims. That claim now carries its scope.
8. **GNoME** is a discovery and scrutiny pair on novelty and utility with the dispute explicit, not a second self-scoring failure. The reanalysis by Leeman et al. (2024) covers both A-Lab and GNoME and should be attributed as its authors' conclusions.
9. **Sinatra et al.** show the highest-impact paper falls at a random position in a career's sequence. That does not show advance cannot be optimized. Delayed and uncertain outcomes are distinguished from measurable decision objectives.
10. **Priority.** Dunbar recorded laboratories in vivo; Nersessian's ethnographies follow research as it proceeds. "First of its kind" is withdrawn. The narrower contribution is a synchronized human and agent action record linked to independently coded decisions and outcomes, after a prior-art search. And the group's notebook agent has no runs; it is designed to produce a record, and that has to be demonstrated.
11. **History.** BACON's limitation was a fixed representation with supplied variables, not one episode; Klahr and Dunbar is evidence about people, not a system. "Generation is cheap" becomes "candidate text is cheap; good experiments, representations, and questions are not." A recorded policy may encode convention or bias, which is why the comparison is needed.
12. **Agent rules are design hypotheses.** An evidence obligation gates the status label of a claim (conjecture, provisional, warranted), never the generation of a conjecture. Chang's iteration is progressive revision of a measurement system, and independent-looking chains can share assumptions. Expected information gain over an expandable set needs a probability model for the expansion, which nobody has yet. "Never a scalar" becomes "report outcomes separately; a decision rule may use explicit utilities with sensitivity analysis."
13. **Reporting.** Twenty-four proposed readings and forty-five reference entries were two different lists; the tiers are made explicit in section 4.2. "All Crossref-verified" is narrowed: every entry with a DOI was registry-checked; the five books without a DOI were not, and are marked for a publisher check before they enter `references.bib`.

### 4.1 Phase 1: the glossary and the ledger. Before meeting 1. Assistant drafts; convenor approves.

**The public page keeps its shape** and gains a status line: the ten headings are teaching entry points; the coding scheme is the three layers below. The prose version goes to `private/text/glossary.md`.

**Layer A, epistemic work.** What a claim owes. Eight families, provisional:

| Family | Owes |
|:--|:--|
| Characterize a phenomenon | Coverage, completeness, consistency, a detection model |
| Estimate an unobserved quantity | Resolution, uncertainty, identifiability, sensitivity to assumptions, recovery of known cases |
| Establish a regularity | Out-of-sample fit, stability across catalogs, survival under completeness and declustering corrections, a stated range |
| Construct a concept or model | Consistency, limiting cases, a consequence later found, unification of separate facts |
| Discriminate explanations | Rivals enumerated (sub-label), a discriminating observable, an outcome that could have failed (sub-label), controls |
| Forecast or simulate | Skill on held-out observables, agreement across structurally different models, a prospective test |
| Reconstruct or integrate | Convergence of independent traces, a discriminating trace, provenance |
| Assess robustness | The conditions that could break the result; an informative failure |

Instruments and methods leave this layer. Building one is an action (Layer B); the claim it makes falls under characterize, estimate, or assess robustness; the contribution is recorded in Layer C.

**Layer B, inquiry actions.** What an agent does and what an episode record logs: inspect; vary conditions or representations; calibrate; build an apparatus or a method; change representation; frame or reframe the question; derive; simulate; seek a discriminating observation; transfer a method across fields; challenge or replicate; consult the literature; defer or abstain; record. Transition conditions from the readings attach here.

**Layer C, context and contribution.** Contribution type (data, method, instrument, estimate, concept, replication, synthesis); aim (understanding, use, both); co-production; infrastructure; intervention or observation; hypothesis stated before the result, as presented; registered before the data; who set the question and from what; whether and when it was reframed; disciplinary resources drawn on; constraints; kind of record.

**Three gates**, defined on the page: action, registration, claim, each with its examples from the reading and what the reading says about it.

**Precedents and the term collision**, one paragraph: Crombie (1994), Hacking (1992), Hey et al. (2009), Gibbons et al. (1994), Kell and Oliver (2004).

**The ledger** (`rubrics.qmd`, `notes.qmd`) gains columns: epistemic work; actions in order; question origin; reframed during the work; registered before the data; and the gate column splits into action, registration, claim. Evidence basis splits into raw artifact, contemporaneous statement, retrospective interpretation.

Check: meeting 1 revises the page; the revised page is the seminar's first output as planned. Decision for the convenor: whether the eight families replace "ten modes" in the corpus codebook outright or run beside the ten in the pilot for comparison. Astra and I both prefer the comparison.

### 4.5 Phase 5: the comparison. After meeting 9, results to meeting 13. Assistant runs; two members score.

Arms: a competent generic planner given the same checks in unstructured form; a hypothesis-testing planner allowed ordinary inspection and calibration; a routing planner using the three layers; a strict action-gate ablation as a fourth arm. Model, tools, data, budget, and human intervention fixed and recorded.

Tasks from the velocity-change project: a credible physical change; a processing artifact; confounded rival mechanisms with one separating observable; a hydrology transfer that is valid only after an assumption check; answerable and insufficient-evidence cases, with abstention credited only where warranted.

Outcomes reported separately: warranted gain; inferential performance; novelty against closest prior art; quality of the next observation; transfer validity; cost; question diversity across runs. Seeds repeated; failures reported.

The analysis plan is written and dated in `gaia-eval` before any run. The seminar applies the registration gate to itself.
