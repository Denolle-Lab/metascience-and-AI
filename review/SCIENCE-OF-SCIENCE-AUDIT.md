# Reading-club audit — September 10, 2026

**Assessment:** The project is a strong starting point for studying plural scientific practices and translating them into scientific agents. Its ten modes are useful discussion prompts, but not yet a validated taxonomy of physical science. The most promising research contribution is to test whether evidence-informed choices between inquiry activities improve scientific outcomes and productive cross-field transfer.

This audit reviews the public project sources, thirteen meeting plans, glossary, agent-design notes, rubrics, corpus proposal, prior-art registry, earlier audits, and bibliography metadata. Targeted primary-source checks underpin the corrections and additions below. It is not a full-text scientific review of every bibliography item or an inspection of the private agent implementation. Recommendations below have not been applied to the curriculum.

**What already works**

The paired readings distinguish philosophical arguments, primary scientific reports, histories, retrospectives, and benchmarks. The project gives observation, instruments, exploration, historical inference, and correction a place alongside hypothesis testing. Its distinction between the research trajectory and the published narrative is essential. The Novelty Dossier and Scientific Advance Profile already separate newness, evidential support, capability gain, and downstream uptake. Those are foundations to retain.

The emphasis has nevertheless drifted toward arguing about hypothesis gates and auditing agents. Your stated aim is wider: understanding how worthwhile scientific advances arise, including through interdisciplinary work. Generation, representation change, question selection, and collective diversity deserve as much attention as verification. Treat agent evaluation as a way to test these ideas, rather than making every club discussion a specification exercise.

**Are the ten modes appropriate?**

Yes as a provisional vocabulary for physical-science activities; no as ten exclusive, exhaustive natural kinds. Their number is an editorial choice that should survive or change through use.

The current rows operate at different levels. Hypothesis testing is an inferential activity; instruments and methods can describe contributions; use-inspired research describes an orientation; co-production describes how research is organized. A community-designed sensor project can simultaneously be use-inspired, co-produced, observational, exploratory, and methodological. The existing allowance for secondary modes helps, but a compulsory single decisive mode still risks manufacturing differences between papers.

For a minimal revision, retain the first nine families, move use orientation and co-production into separate attributes, and use the tenth family for **problem finding and question framing**. This is a proposed operational vocabulary, not an assertion that science intrinsically contains ten modes:

| Activity family | Refinement and agent capability | Evidence that the capability worked |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Observe and characterize | Establish distributions, classifications, coverage, and phenomena; maintain detection and sampling models. | Recover known signals and identify coverage-driven apparent patterns. |
| Explore patterns and anomalies | Vary representations and conditions; retain surprises even when a broader hypothesis exists. | Find reproducible structure without converting exploratory selection into confirmation. |
| Generate and discriminate explanations | Include abduction and rival generation as explicit subactivities, followed by suitable tests. | Identify observations that distinguish live rivals; recognize when they do not. |
| Construct theory and mechanisms | Include mathematical derivation, analogy, idealization, symmetry, scaling, and changes of representation. | Check consistency, limiting cases, and new consequences; identify failed analogies. |
| Simulate, estimate, and predict | Distinguish inverse estimation, scenario exploration, and prospective forecasting. | Assess identifiability, uncertainty, numerical error, and performance outside calibration conditions. |
| Develop and characterize observing systems | Include measurement-chain diagnosis, calibration, and adaptive sensing. | Separate instrument response from physical change using independent evidence. |
| Develop methods and computation | Build inferential or computational capabilities; record assumptions and operating regimes. | Recover known cases, expose artifacts, and compare independent implementations. |
| Synthesize and reconstruct | Keep historical reconstruction and cross-literature synthesis as distinct subactivities. | Check trace provenance, shared dependencies, competing histories, and transfer assumptions. |
| Replicate, challenge, and assess robustness | Distinguish computational reproduction, empirical replication, and transfer. | Find genuine failures while accepting clean controls; report what the failure teaches. |
| Find and frame questions | Turn an uncertainty, anomaly, new capability, or practical need into tractable competing research directions. | Propose questions whose answers would change knowledge or capability, with feasible next observations and explicit opportunity costs. |

Use separate attributes for intervention/observation; exploratory/confirmatory status; epistemic/applied aims; co-production; infrastructure; discipline integration; and contribution role. Episodes may receive several activity labels. Papers can then be summarized by their activity profile; a decisive move can remain an interpretive note.

Several current recognition rules need loosening:

- Exploration does not require the absence of every prior hypothesis. An exploratory branch can arise inside a targeted study. Meeting 2 recognizes this better than the glossary does.
- A derivation need not precede observations for work to be theoretical. Theory can reorganize established facts.
- Historical reconstruction and synthesis can use new interventions, including laboratory experiments that constrain trace-forming processes.
- Repeating an event is not always possible. A transient or a past event can warrant follow-up through independent records, calibration, or distinct traces.
- Breadth and depth are separate dimensions. Topic concentration is a specialization proxy, not a direct measure of competence; an expert can be both broad and deep.
- Use-inspired research is not necessarily co-produced, and co-production need not be reduced to usefulness after publication.

For generality beyond geoscience, test the vocabulary on theoretical physics, astronomy, experimental condensed matter, chemistry/materials synthesis, and metrology. Include routine work and unsuccessful projects alongside celebrated discoveries. Add mathematical reasoning and construction/synthesis as visible subactivities before deciding that extra top-level modes are needed.

**Specific corrections and qualifications**

1. **Wrong citation attached to an execution-study claim.** `agent-design.qmd:38` names arXiv:2506.20803 but cites `si2025`, which is the distinct ICLR idea-rating study. Add a separate record for *The Ideation–Execution Gap*. Its 43-researcher execution study found a larger decline in ratings for AI ideas after execution; this supports a bounded ideation-versus-execution distinction, not the statement that LLMs generally generate hypotheses badly. Sources: [execution study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.20803), [ICLR ideation study](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/ea94957d81b1c1caf87ef5319fa6b467-Abstract-Conference.html).

2. **Oreskes is summarized incorrectly.** `agent-design.qmd:51` says open-system models cannot be confirmed. The paper explicitly permits partial confirmation through agreement between prediction and observation, while rejecting complete confirmation. Suggested replacement: “Agreement can partially support a model; it does not establish complete truth or unrestricted validity.” The meeting-9 page is already more accurate. [Original article, university-hosted copy](https://web.pdx.edu/~changh/Oreskes%20et%20al.1994.pdf).

3. **Karaca has a relevant erratum.** It corrects equations, references, and a footnote about Bjorken's scaling hypothesis preceding deep-inelastic-scattering experiments. That chronology matters to this club's central question. Keep the paper, with its [2013 erratum](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0269889713000215).

4. **Ioannidis does not establish a universal action gate.** Meeting 6 calls it the strongest published case for a hypothesis gate. Its formal argument concerns the reliability of claimed statistically significant relationships under assumptions about prior odds, power, bias, and multiple testing. A predeclared hypothesis alone does not solve those problems. Teach it as a challenge to evidential claims after flexible search. [Original paper](https://journals.plos.org/plosmedicine/article?id=10.1371/journal.pmed.0020124).

5. **Turn empirical patterns into candidate policies, not universal rules.** “Pursue an anomaly only after it reproduces” in `agent-design.qmd:46` can exclude unique earthquakes and astronomical transients. “Seek independent corroboration or characterize the measurement before escalating the claim” is more portable. Following up an anomaly may be how its reliability is established.

6. **Avoid unsupported universal comparisons.** Claims that no benchmark rewards abstention, every benchmark supplies the question, a generic research agent necessarily conflates evidence types, or the corpus variable has never been measured need bounded searches or direct experiments. Phrase them as findings within a dated, explicitly coded comparison set. Distinguish broad goal assignment from choosing a subquestion, hypothesis, variable, or next measurement. The existing Co-Scientist paper also deserves consideration in that comparison. [Co-Scientist, 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10644-y).

7. **Individual breadth and collective breadth are different outcomes.** Hao et al. is highly relevant, but its association between AI adoption and a narrower collective focus does not itself falsify the claim that some individuals acquire broader competence. Doshi–Hauser's creative-writing experiment is a transfer analogy, not an experiment in scientific discovery. [Hao et al., 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09922-y).

8. **Do not turn all warrant into one universal test form.** “No claim without a test it could have failed” usefully disciplines empirical explanations, but derivations, descriptive claims, capability demonstrations, and provisional conjectures owe different evidence. Require warrant appropriate to the claim, including explicit conjectural status when warranted evidence is not yet available.

**How to turn the modes into scientific agents**

Treat modes as selectable procedures with evidence requirements, not as ten personalities. Start by adding procedures to the existing orchestrator. Creating more agents is a separate engineering decision, requiring evidence that division of labor helps.

The shared research state should contain the current question; observations and provenance; measurement assumptions; representations; live explanations; uncertainties; prior-art findings; decisions; and the remaining resource budget. Each proposed action records its purpose, expected evidential consequence, cost, result, and why the next action changed. Explicit logs and tool outputs are the research record; generated rationales are not automatically reliable descriptions of internal reasoning.

An initial policy can be small: inspect data and measurement provenance; explore alternative representations; identify consequential unknowns; propose several explanations or questions; choose a discriminating or enabling action; revise, synthesize, or abstain. Allow returns to earlier activities and expansion of the candidate model set. Expected information gain within a fixed set cannot recognize an explanation outside that set.

Make cross-field transfer an explicit operation. Record the source field's mechanism or method, the target problem, the mapping of quantities and assumptions, where that mapping fails, and a cheap discriminating check. A list of unrelated citations is not successful integration. Different prompting roles applied to the same model are also not evidence of independent expertise.

The objective should be **warranted gains in knowledge, understanding, or capability under a resource budget**, with novelty and breadth reported separately. At project level, compare whether useful new questions and results emerge. At portfolio level, compare diversity of questions, mechanisms, evidence sources, and methods. Preserve some resources for uncertain directions; do not require every exploratory action to promise an immediate discovery. Report tradeoffs rather than selecting arbitrary scalar weights for novelty, impact, and diversity.

**A research opportunity, with a tractable first experiment**

There is a credible opportunity here. The broad idea of studying discovery to improve AI has precedents already in the reading list. A stronger contribution would be a validated account of inquiry decisions that demonstrably improves modern agent behavior in open, noisy physical systems.

Start with the mechanism: “Does choosing inquiry activities using the current evidence improve scientific progress compared with an otherwise matched generic planner?” Add the breadth hypothesis separately: “Does explicit cross-field transfer yield useful new tests or explanations while preserving evidential quality?” Neither hypothesis follows just from recognizing multiple scientific methods.

Use three connected stages:

1. **Observe practice.** Reconstruct a small set of local projects from dated records, including failures and routine work. Supplement these with a few contrasting physical-science cases. Code actions, representations, constraints, uncertainty, and transitions. Have independent readers code episodes; revise the vocabulary where they disagree. Observe some ongoing work prospectively to reduce hindsight bias.
2. **Build a small evaluation.** Use one or two task families: a measurement anomaly, rival mechanisms with similar fits, and a cross-field method-transfer opportunity. Include real data plus controlled perturbations where useful. Include both answerable and insufficient-evidence cases. Historical replay is useful for teaching, but familiar discoveries may already be in model training.
3. **Compare policies.** Hold model, tools, data access, human assistance, and budgets fixed. Compare a competent generic planner, a hypothesis-testing planner, and an activity-routing planner. Then ablate explicit mode labels/routing and the cross-field transfer procedure. Repeat across seeds and held-out cases; report failures as well as successful runs.

For the seasonal seismic-velocity example, test whether the system distinguishes an instrument/processing change from a physical change, identifies a useful observable separating groundwater and thermoelastic explanations, and checks the assumptions of a borrowed hydrological method. A correct “not distinguishable with these observations” should receive credit only on cases that warrant it. Penalize unnecessary abstention on answerable cases.

Primary outcomes can be externally checked evidential quality, discriminating value of the next observation, new capability, and cost to a defensible result. Novelty requires closest-prior-art comparison. Question diversity requires checking substantive distinctions rather than wording. A small pilot can establish feasibility and expose failure modes; it cannot establish long-term scientific impact or settle which architecture is universally best.

**Changes needed before scaling the corpus study**

The proposed corpus study is valuable for describing how papers present their contributions. It cannot establish how scientists actually worked or whether a mode caused impact.

- Use episodes for process claims and papers for published contribution profiles. Preserve unknown chronology. Full text helps, but does not recover an undocumented process.
- Add a physical-science comparison corpus before using molecular biology as the only external comparison. Journal membership alone does not isolate seismology in broad geophysics journals.
- Replace a compulsory single mode with multi-label coding and an explicit ambiguous/uncodable outcome. Check whether modes improve prediction or interpretation beyond simpler attributes such as new data, intervention, and contribution type.
- Stratify human validation by field, period, access status, and rare activities. Three hundred examples in total may leave individual modes almost untested. Include abstention, calibration, confusion, and prevalence correction; quantify uncertainty introduced by classification.
- Do not use absence of an explicit hypothesis in an abstract as evidence that no hypothesis guided the work. Redacting authors and venues reduces some cues but cannot blind recognizable papers fully.
- Separate specialization from competence. Reference diversity and concentration can be mathematically related; entering them as independent measures of breadth and depth risks circular conclusions.
- Use comparable citation windows and examine access/abstract missingness, article type, infrastructure, team characteristics, and field conventions. Distinguish confounders from possible mediators before adjusting for them. Year and subfield controls alone do not identify causal effects.
- Treat retrospective “field-shaping” cases as selected cases. Add ordinary, failed, and enabling contributions to avoid learning only from survivors.
- Replace “not measured” with a documented prior-art search result. Search empirical studies of scientific practice, research-strategy taxonomies, epistemic cultures, discovery cognition, and research-method classification, not only science-agent benchmarks.

The most useful immediate deliverable would be a dozen well-documented inquiry episodes and a small controlled policy comparison. Those would tell you whether the taxonomy carries actionable information before committing to large-scale paper classification.
